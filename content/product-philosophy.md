# Product philosophy

## "AI understands me when I need it" — not "AI continuously analyses me"

Habitty should feel intelligent because it has the right context at the right moment, not because it is constantly producing commentary. Every AI feature had to justify itself against a simpler, deterministic alternative.

## Principles

### Structure without rigidity
Each habit has **minimum / target / stretch** levels. Reaching the minimum counts fully. On transition days, short-sleep days or recovery days, the plan automatically lowers what the day expects — it never raises the bar on a hard day.

### Guidance before tracking
Today opens with a short briefing: one sentence for *right now*, a few next steps, and what to avoid — built from rules that understand the shift and the personal day. Logging is one tap away, but the screen leads with direction, not with forms.

### Capability over streaks
There are no streak counters to protect. Progress is measured as consistency over weeks ("habits touched"), with targets and stretch recorded but never required.

### Context instead of isolated data
Sleep, meals, training, supplements, tasks, goals, values, decisions and calendar events are connected through one **context builder**. A question like "Should I train today?" is answered against the actual shift, sleep and upcoming race — not in isolation.

### Complexity behind the scenes, simplicity at the surface
Shift resolution, personal-day boundaries, meal timing relative to sleep, supplement due windows, relevance-gated AI context and rate limiting are all invisible. The user sees a calm page with a few clear actions.

### Reflection without productivity scores
Snapshot asks "what actually happened?"; emotions are recorded as words, not scores; Progress shows change, not grades. The weekly review and reports describe the week; they never rank the person.

### The user stays in charge
Every suggestion can be added as a task, snoozed or dismissed for the day. AI interpretations are labelled as interpretations and can be corrected; corrections are remembered as the user's.

### No new information → no new analysis
If a worry has already been thought through and nothing material has changed, the system says so instead of generating fresh analysis. This principle, born in the Decision Tree, shaped the whole AI design.

### Know when not to use AI
Averages, counts, trends, time zones, reminders, conflicts and plans are calculated in code. AI is reserved for interpretation, synthesis and language.
